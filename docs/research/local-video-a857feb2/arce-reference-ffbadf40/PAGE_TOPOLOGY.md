## Visual system

- Canvas: warm paper `#f4f3ef`; ink `#191916`; border `#cfcdc5`; signal red `#e2291c`.
- Typography: local display and sans placeholders with serif italic contrast; final brand fonts remain replaceable.
- Grid: three equal columns for product browsing, hairline separators, low-density editorial spacing.
- Imagery: solid neutral, tea, mineral and oxide colour fields only. No synthetic product illustrations are introduced.
